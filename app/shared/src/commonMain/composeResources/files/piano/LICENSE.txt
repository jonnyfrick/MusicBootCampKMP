The piano samples in this folder are derived from the

    Salamander Grand Piano V3
    by Alexander Holm
    https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
    https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano

licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported License
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Changes: one velocity layer (v10) only; mixed to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz / 16 bit,
trimmed to the first seconds of each note with a fade-out, scaled by a common factor and
encoded as FLAC (see tools/prepare_piano_samples.py in the MusicBootCampKMP repository).
